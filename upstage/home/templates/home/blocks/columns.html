{% comment %} A columns block: two, three or four columns side by side, each with an image over its text. On phones the columns stack, in order. Normally the columns share the block's card. With separate_columns each column is its own card, with a gap between and no card round them all. With equal_height the columns are as tall as the tallest: drawn in outlined boxes if they share the card, or just stretched if they are separate cards. With flush_images the image of a card or box runs to its edges and only the text is padded (the card's padding moves to the text).
